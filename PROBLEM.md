# Real-Time Urban Flood Inundation Mapping and Evacuation Router

**Domain:** Smart Cities

**Complexity:** 5/5

**Description:**

Urban flooding displaces millions annually, and city emergency managers lack real-time, street-level inundation maps during active flood events. Build a platform that fuses rainfall radar data, IoT water-level sensor streams, OpenStreetMap elevation profiles, and crowdsourced social media reports to generate a live flood depth map updated every 5 minutes. The system should auto-generate dynamic, flood-avoiding evacuation routes for emergency services and citizens, push alerts to geo-fenced mobile users, and produce an incident timeline exportable for post-disaster insurance and government audits.
